# Privacy Policy for Sudoku

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

This policy explains how the Sudoku app ("the App") for iPhone and iPad handles your information.

## Summary

**The App does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information.**

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following only on your device, inside the App's private storage:

- Your saved games (puzzle board, your entries and notes, elapsed time)
- Your statistics (games played, games solved, completion times, and the dates you solved puzzles for daily streaks)

This information never leaves your device. The developer cannot access it. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's privacy policy covers.

You can delete this information at any time:
- Abandon a saved game from the game's ⋯ menu or the main screen
- Reset your statistics from the Statistics screen
- Delete the App to remove all of its data

## Information We Do Not Collect

The App:
- Has no user accounts or sign-in
- Makes no network connections
- Contains no advertising
- Contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party tracking tools
- Does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal data
- Does not track you across other apps or websites

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).
